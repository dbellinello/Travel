✅ Pre-Ship Checklist

Phase: 5 — Ship gate

Summary: The final self-audit Claude runs before marking any guide build done. Answer each question honestly. Any "no" means the guide is not ready to ship — fix the gap and re-run the checklist.

§ 1. When to run

Before marking a guide build "done" — every time. Not optional. § 10 includes running the validator as part of this checklist — answer every section before reaching it.

This includes patch sessions. Any session that modifies guide HTML runs this checklist and the validator before saying done. The scope of the change is not an exemption.

Resuming an incomplete build: read _build/build_state.md before doing anything. An unchecked Phase 5 means the guide is not done. Complete Phase 5 before any done claim.

§ 2. Build-state tracker

The tracker at Guides/{City}/_build/build_state.md exists and every [ ] for Phases 1-4 is flipped to [x]? Any unchecked phase reads means a file was skipped — go read it before continuing.

§ 3. Research & selection

  • Was the skip list checked first (Brain/mds/Cities Skip List.md at the ## [City] heading)?
  • Every candidate came from a trusted source — Fodor's, Culture Trip, Rough Guides, Atlas Obscura, Rick Steves, National Geographic, or the official tourism board?
  • No blogs, no AI SEO content, no affiliate listicles, no user-generated lists?
  • No top-10 thinking — fame alone wasn't a reason to include?
  • Meaningful over full — no padded days with filler picks?

§ 4. Day shape

  • Each day opens with the 🏨 FROM HOTEL: banner?
  • Each day closes with the hotel return?
  • Stop count meets the rule: ≥4 for full self-guided days, with down to 1 permitted when a single distant or all-day stop sets the day's pace (per Day Structure.html § 5)?
  • Train Day quota satisfied: ≥5-day guide includes at least one Train Day; ≤4-day guide includes none (per Day Structure.html §§ 6–7)?
  • Geographic clustering — one loop or one path, no backtracking?

§ 5. Format

  • Title page: three lines (CITY uppercase / Hotel or Rental name / Address)?
  • Every stop block follows the icon order in Icon Order and Format.html?
  • Every motion banner follows Motion Rule.html (opener, between-stop banners, and day closer all present and correct)?
  • Every 📍 address row follows Links.html § 6 format: {Street} · {Neighborhood} — no postal code or country; the address text itself is the clickable destination anchor (no separate token)?
  • Every 🎟 ticket-box follows the format in Tickets.html?
  • Toolbar present: the toolbar-mount div + toolbar.js script are the first items inside the container, with data-depth="2" and data-maxwidth="940" (per Brain/Reference/Toolbar.html; enforced by validator TB-1 through TB-8)?
  • Footer sharing handled by toolbar.js — guide loads toolbar.js and has no inline footer div of its own (per Brain/Reference/Toolbar.html § 5; enforced by validator TB-9)?

§ 6. Photos

  • Every stop has a photo harvested in the same pass as the stop research (not deferred)?
  • Every photo is from Wikimedia Commons with an acceptable license (Public Domain · CC0 · CC-BY · CC-BY-SA)?
  • Every photo file is stored locally in Guides/{City}/_build/assets/ — no hotlinking to upload.wikimedia.org?
  • Stops without a usable Commons image ship the "No pictures found" line, not silent omission?

§ 7. URLs

  • Every URL returns HTTP 200?
  • Every URL page carries ≥100 words of editorial content?
  • Bot-blocked platforms (Viator, GetYourGuide, TheFork, Michelin, TripAdvisor) verified via site: search-result inspection?
  • Verification log present at Guides/{City}/_build/verification_log.json?
  • Every external anchor carries target="_blank"?

§ 8. Extra sections

Every extra section is present and either populated or carries its negative-finding line. Order must match this canonical sequence:

  • 🗓️ Weekly Closures
  • 📅 Tours
  • ☕ Cappuccino
  • 🫕 Restaurants Near Hotel
  • 🍽️ Downtown Restaurants
  • 🍮 Local Tastes
  • 🚗 Food Delivery
  • 🎭 Shows, Performances & Concerts
  • 🚌 Getting Around
  • 🚆 Train Stations Near Hotel
  • ⛲️ Day Trips by Train
  • 🏓 Pickleball (California, Arizona, and Oregon only — silently omitted elsewhere)
  • ⭐ Michelin Restaurants
  • ❗️ Heads Up (conditional — ships only when entries exist)
  • 🌟 Claude Inspiration (optional — silently omitted when nothing surfaced)
  • Skip List footnote (conditional — very last; ships only when the city has a skip list)

§ 9. Data integrity

  • Every factual row (🏛 hours, 🚶 walk, 🚕 ride, ⏰ duration, 📍 address, 🎟 booking) comes from a confirmed source — venue's own site, Google Places, or a verifiable third-party listing?
  • No hedging language ("typically open," "usually," "approximately," "around," etc.) in any factual row?
  • No fabricated data — every fact live-verified this build, not pulled from memory or past guides?
  • No money symbols ($, €, £, ¥, ISO codes) anywhere in the shipped guide?
  • No tilde (~) anywhere in the guide — banned as a hedge/approximation marker outside ⏰ timing rows?
  • No placeholders ({TBD}, {TODO}, "fill in later")?

§ 10. Validator

  • python3 Brain/scripts/brain_check.py passes? (Run separately — not in the ship chain)
  • python3 Brain/scripts/guide_tools.py ship [Guide] passes? (Chains validate_itinerary + verify_urls + verify_booking_links in one command)

§ 11. Post-ship

All five steps below happen in a single pass. Partial updates break the chain and block the validator.

  • New card added in the correct region section, in canonical order, with the right data-guide-num?
  • Predecessor card's data-guide-next and successor card's data-guide-prev updated to point to the new guide?
  • Banner destination count and region guide count both updated?
  • The guide file's own toolbar-mount data-prev and data-next set to match its neighbours in the chain?
  • City pin added to Trip Essentials/Maps/Europe Map.html (European guides) or Trip Essentials/Maps/US Map.html (US guides)? Entry format in the PINS array: ['CityName', lon, lat, '../Guides/City/file.html']. The validator and ship gate both hard-fail if this is missing. Rule: Navigation.html § 5 step 5.
