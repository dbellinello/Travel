Phase: 4 — Per-section build

Summary: Train day-trip destinations from the guide city.

§ 1. What ships

Search day trips from [City] across reputable travel guides — Fodor's, Culture Trip, Rough Guides, Rick Steves, National Geographic, and the official tourism board — and add every destination they recommend. These sources already filter for quality and define what counts as a day trip, so trust their picks. A destination not recommended by any of these sources doesn't ship.

§ 2. Entry Format(s)

The country is added only when the destination is across the border — in a different country from the destination [City]. No postal code.
