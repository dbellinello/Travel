Day 1
🏨 FROM HOTEL: 🚶 25 min · 🚕 10 min → Rijksmuseum
1.Rijksmuseum
📒 The greatest collection of Dutch Golden Age art on earth. Rembrandt's Night Watch and Vermeer's Milkmaid anchor a museum so comprehensive it defines Amsterdam as a cultural destination. The 19th-century building is itself a monument.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Rijksmuseum · 4.8⭐ · rijksmuseum.nl
🏛️ Daily 9:00am - 5:00pm
⏰ ~2 hrs 30 min
📍 Museumstraat 1 · Museum Quarter
🚶 3 min · 🚕 3 min → Van Gogh Museum
2.Van Gogh Museum
📒 The world's largest Van Gogh collection — over 200 paintings and 500 drawings tracing his entire career in one building. Almond Blossom · Sunflowers · The Bedroom: the real canvases. No comparable concentration of this artist's work exists anywhere else.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Van Gogh Museum · 4.8⭐ · vangoghmuseum.nl
🏛️ Daily 9:00am - 6:00pm
⏰ ~1 hr 30 min
⚠️ Timed-entry tickets sell out weeks ahead — book online before arriving
📍 Museumplein 6 · Museum Quarter
🚶 4 min · 🚕 3 min → Vondelpark
3.Vondelpark
📒 Amsterdam's beloved 47-acre park — the city's living room. Landscaped in English garden style with canals · rose gardens · and a Victorian pavilion. Free open-air concerts run June to August on the main stage. The perfect reset between two museum giants.
📖 Wikipedia
🏛️ Open 24/7
⏰ ~45 min
📍 Stadhouderskade · Museum Quarter
🚶 28 min · 🚕 13 min → hotel
Day 2
🏨 FROM HOTEL: 🚶 30 min · 🚕 12 min → Anne Frank House
1.Anne Frank House
📒 The actual hiding place where Anne Frank wrote her diary for two years during the Nazi occupation. The Secret Annex · her room · the bookcase door — all preserved. One of the most affecting historical sites in Europe · told on a deeply human scale.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Anne Frank House · 4.8⭐ · annefrank.org
🏛️ Daily 9:00am - 10:00pm
⏰ ~1 hr 30 min
⚠️ Tickets released every Tuesday at 10:00am Amsterdam time · six weeks ahead · sell out in hours during peak season
📍 Westermarkt 20 · Jordaan
🚶 1 min · 🚕 1 min → Westerkerk
2.Westerkerk
📒 Amsterdam's grandest Protestant church · built 1620–1631 by Hendrick de Keyser. Rembrandt is buried inside — exact location lost to history. The 85m Westertoren with its Imperial Crown dominates the Jordaan skyline and was visible from Anne Frank's window in the Annex.
📖 Wikipedia
🏛️ Mon–Fri 11:00am - 3:00pm
🚫 Closed Saturday
🚫 Closed Sunday
🆓 Free
⏰ ~30 min
⚠️ Tower closed for restoration — church nave open
📍 Prinsengracht 281 · Jordaan
🚶 3 min · 🚕 2 min → Negen Straatjes
3.Negen Straatjes
📒 Nine historic cross-streets threading the Jordaan canal ring — Amsterdam's finest pedestrian stroll. Independent boutiques · specialty food shops · and terrace restaurants tucked into 17th-century canal-house ground floors. The antidote to every tourist-trap shopping street in the city.
📖 Wikipedia
🏛️ Daily — shops mostly 10:00am - 6:00pm

Day 3
🏨 FROM HOTEL: 🚶 5 min · 🚕 3 min → Hortus Botanicus
1.Hortus Botanicus Amsterdam
📒 One of the world's oldest botanical gardens · founded 1638 as a medicinal herb garden. Holds 6·000 plant species across three climate greenhouses including a 300-year-old Eastern Cape cycad and the coffee plant that seeded all of Latin America's coffee culture.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Hortus Botanicus Amsterdam · 4.5⭐ · dehortus.nl
🏛️ Daily 10:00am - 5:00pm
⏰ ~1 hr
📍 Plantage Middenlaan 2a · De Plantage
🚶 6 min · 🚕 3 min → Portuguese Synagogue
2.Portuguese Synagogue
📒 Built in 1675 and still lit by 1·000 candles — no electricity was ever installed. One of the largest and best-preserved synagogues in the world. Sand-covered floors and towering Tuscan columns unchanged since the Sephardic community built it after fleeing the Inquisition.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Portuguese Synagogue · 4.7⭐ · esnoga.com
🏛️ Sun–Fri 10:00am - 5:00pm
🚫 Closed Saturday
⏰ ~45 min
⚠️ Dress code required — shoulders covered · skirts to knee · men wear a kippa
📍 Mr. Visserplein 3 · Jewish Quarter
🚶 10 min · 🚕 5 min → NEMO Science Museum
3.NEMO Science Museum
📒 Renzo Piano's copper-clad ship hull rises from the IJ waterfront — the building is a landmark before you enter. The free rooftop terrace has a panoramic view over the old port and city rooftops. Five floors of interactive science exhibits inside.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ NEMO Science Museum · 4.5⭐ · nemosciencemuseum.nl
🏛️ Tue–Sun 10:00am - 5:00pm
🚫 Closed Monday
⏰ ~1 hr 30 min
📍 Oosterdok 2 · Centrum
🚶 17 min · 🚕 7 min → Begijnhof
4.Begijnhof
📒 A hidden medieval courtyard entered through an unmarked door in the city center. Dating to 1346 · it housed the Beguines — lay religious women under vows of chastity. The oldest surviving wooden house in the city — No. 34 · built c. 1420 — stands here.
📖 Wikipedia
🏛️ Daily 9:00am - 5:00pm
🆓 Free
⏰ ~40 min
⚠️ Quiet courtyard — speak softly; do not photograph private dwellings
📍 Begijnhof 30 · Centrum
🚶 22 min · 🚕 10 min → hotel

🏨 FROM HOTEL: 🚶 10 min · 🚕 5 min → Jewish Historical Museum
1.Jewish Historical Museum
📒 Four interconnected 17th and 18th-century Ashkenazi synagogues — the Great Synagogue · Obbene Shul · Dritt Shul · and Neie Shul — form the Netherlands' most comprehensive Jewish museum. Documents 400 years of Jewish life · culture · and survival in the city.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Jewish Historical Museum · 4.5⭐ · jck.nl
🏛️ Daily 11:00am - 5:00pm
⏰ ~1 hr 30 min
📍 Nieuwe Amstelstraat 1 · Jewish Quarter

2.EYE Filmmuseum
📒 A dramatic white deconstructivist building cantilevered over the IJ waterfront — striking from the free ferry crossing and every angle on shore. Inside: one of Europe's finest film archives · rotating cinema exhibitions · and a panoramic view back across the river to the city skyline.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ EYE Filmmuseum · 4.5⭐ · eyefilm.nl
🏛️ Tue–Sun 10:00am - 7:00pm
🚫 Closed Monday
⏰ ~1 hr
📍 IJpromenade 1 · Noord
🚶 5 min · 🚕 3 min → A'DAM Lookout

📒 A 360° observation deck on the 20th floor of the former Shell Tower · with a swing that launches over the edge 100 meters above the IJ. The panorama sweeps across the full canal city — every church tower visible and the countryside beyond on clear days.

🏛️ Daily 10:00am - 10:00pm
⏰ ~45 min

1.Dom Tower
📒 The tallest church tower in the Netherlands at 112 meters · built 1321–1382. The 465-step guided climb passes through medieval belfry rooms · the clock mechanism · and the 50-bell carillon · ending in a panorama over Utrecht and the Dutch countryside.
📖 Wikipedia
🎟️ Dom Tower · 4.7⭐ · domtoren.nl
🏛️ Daily 10:00am - 5:00pm
⏰ ~1 hr 30 min
⚠️ Guided tours only · 75 min · 465 steps · book ahead online
📍 Domplein · Old Town
🚶 3 min · 🚕 2 min → Oudegracht
2.Oudegracht
📒 Utrecht's medieval canal runs two levels deep — an upper street and lower wharf cellars cut directly into the canal banks for unloading ships. Today the cellars are cafés and restaurants open to the water. No other city has this two-level structure.
📖 Wikipedia
🏛️ Open 24/7
⏰ ~1 hr

🍮 Local Tastes
Bitterballen
Deep-fried crispy ragout balls served hot with Dutch mustard — the essential bar snack. Beef or veal bound in thick béchamel · panko-crusted · always served in an odd number.
📍 Eik & Linde · Café de Klos · Café Luxembourg
Hollandse Nieuwe Haring
Raw herring sliced into pieces with raw onion and pickles · eaten with a toothpick. The season runs May to July; the first barrel goes to the King.
📍 Stubbe's Haring · Frens Haringhandel
Stroopwafel
Two thin waffle discs sandwiched with caramel syrup — best hot off the iron at a market stall where the syrup is still molten.
📍 Albert Cuyp Market
Jenever
Dutch gin — ancestor of London dry · distilled from malt wine and juniper. Served in a tulip glass filled to the brim; lower your head to the glass for the first sip.
📍 Wynand Fockink · Proeflokaal De Ooievaar
Poffertjes
Tiny fluffy buckwheat pancakes cooked in a cast-iron mould · served in a heap of 15–20 with butter and powdered sugar. A Dutch fairground food elevated to café staple.
📍 Pancake Bakery · Upstairs Pancake House

⛲️ Day Trips by Train
Haarlem · 20 min
🗞️ One of the best-preserved medieval cities in the Netherlands. The Frans Hals Museum holds the world's greatest collection of Dutch Golden Age group portraits. The Grote Markt is among the finest medieval squares in Northern Europe.
🎫 book at: ns.nl or omio
Utrecht · 30 min
🗞️ The cathedral city with the Dom Tower — tallest in the Netherlands — and the Oudegracht · a medieval canal whose wharf cellars are now café-restaurants. Intimate and far less crowded than the capital.
🎫 book at: ns.nl or omio
Rotterdam · 40 min
🗞️ Europe's largest port and boldest modern skyline — the Cube Houses · Markthal · and Depot Boijmans define a city completely rebuilt after WWII. It looks like no other Dutch city.
🎫 book at: ns.nl or omio
Delft · 1 hr
🗞️ The city of Vermeer and blue-and-white Delftware. Canals · gabled houses · and a fairy-tale Markt — the Nieuwe Kerk and Prinsenhof flank the square where Willem van Oranje was assassinated in 1584.
🎫 book at: ns.nl or omio
