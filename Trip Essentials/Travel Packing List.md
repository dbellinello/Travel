## 🎒 Bags & Containers

The pre-packed kits and bags that save brain power. Keep doubles inside the toiletry and meds bags so you only have to think when something runs low.

### Bags

- [ ] Carry-on suitcase or backpack
- [ ] Small crossbody bag — daily city walking, hands-free

### Pre-packed kits (just grab and go)

- [ ] Meds bag — all meds together — quick check before trip
- [ ] Toiletries bag — doubles already inside — quick check what's running low
- [ ] First aid — Band-Aids, antiseptic wipes, basics

> **Toiletries bag exception: BAR SOAP.** Hotel body wash is intentionally watery to make you use more. Bring 1 bar soap **per hotel** (not per night) — change hotels = bring a new bar.

## 👕 Clothes

Daily wear scaled to trip length, with weather-fallback rules so you're never caught off guard.

### Daily wear (enough for trip days)

- [ ] Bras
- [ ] Underwear
- [ ] Black socks
- [ ] White sports socks — doubles for pickleball!
- [ ] Enough shirts
- [ ] Enough pants
- [ ] PJs
- [ ] 1 jacket

### Weather fallback (always, even if forecast disagrees!)

- [ ] At least 1 pair of pants — cold-weather backup
- [ ] At least 1 pair of capris — hot-weather backup

### Cold weather kit (always pack!)

> *Paris lesson: Forgot the gloves, scarf, and beanie. Evenings got chilly. Now this trio packs every time, regardless of forecast!*

- [ ] Scarf
- [ ] Gloves
- [ ] Beanie — warm hat
